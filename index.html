04 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Every update links to the original publisher and carries a publication date. Summaries are short, original paraphrases. No copyrighted article images are reproduced.

  1. Collect from identifiable newsrooms, humanitarian bodies or public institutions.
  2. Check the date, attribution, context and whether the claim is reporting or an allegation.
  3. Seek independent corroboration for disputed claims; explicitly label uncertainty.
  4. Review translations and links manually before publication.
  5. Record corrections transparently and update the editorial review date.
  6. Never publish operational details that could endanger civilians or aid workers.

This is an editorial pilot, not a live alert service. Contact and corrections: open an issue in the public repository once deployed. No donations are collected.
